# F. Commercial readiness (SF-159)

Readiness of every commercial surface the Fighter Mobile design draws. All
amounts on the boards are mock data. "Implementation readiness" says whether
mobile could start building against a real contract; "Production" is the
release decision.

No fighter-facing commercial surface is ready for production. The only real
commercial code path is gym SaaS billing, which is gym-owner only and itself
NO-GO for Live (SF-138).

## What mobile may build now

- Nothing that takes money or implies a purchase.
- Gym member products could be **listed** (`listMyGymProducts`) and a
  `pending_activation` request shown honestly, but only after a product
  decision on the copy (D-SF159-16). Until then F16, GC1–GC12 stay BLOCKED.
- Placeholders stay as they are. Commerce routes (`mobile.checkout`,
  `mobile.pro`, `mobile.marketplace`, store routes) keep their "Planned"
  placeholder.
