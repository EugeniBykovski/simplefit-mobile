# H. Decisions and blockers register (SF-159)

Every decision, blocker and gap found by the SF-159 audit, with a stable id.
Types:

- **Approved decision**: already decided, with a reference. Work must follow it.
- **Decision needed**: not decided; blocks or shapes work.
- **Technical blocker**: missing code, contract or check.
- **Design gap**: the Claude Design artifact is missing, inconsistent or
  contradicts the code.
- **External provider blocker**: depends on a third party.
- **Legal / commercial dependency**: needs counsel or a commercial owner.

Owners are proposed, not assigned. "Product" means the product owner;
"Design" means Claude Design changes made by the design owner (SF-159 did
not modify the artifact).
